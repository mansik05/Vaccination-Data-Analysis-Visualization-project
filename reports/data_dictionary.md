# Data Dictionary

The field definitions below follow the supplied project specification.

## coverage
Country/group, ISO-3 code, country name, year, antigen, antigen description, coverage category, target number, doses and coverage percentage.

## incidence_rate
Country/group, ISO-3 code, country name, year, disease, disease description, denominator and incidence rate.

## reported_cases
Country/group, ISO-3 code, country name, year, disease, disease description and reported cases.

## vaccine_introduction
ISO-3 country code, country name, WHO region, year, vaccine description and introduction indicator.

## vaccine_schedule
ISO-3 country code, country name, WHO region, year, vaccine code/description, schedule round, target population, geography, age administered and source comment.
