Core Measures

Total Countries = DISTINCTCOUNT(coverage[code])

Average Coverage % = AVERAGE(coverage[coverage])

Total Doses = SUM(coverage[doses])

Total Target Population = SUM(coverage[target_number])

Coverage Gap to 95% =
MAX(0, 95 - [Average Coverage %])

Total Reported Cases = SUM(reported_cases[cases])

Average Incidence Rate = AVERAGE(incidence_rate[incidence_rate])

Vaccine Introductions =
CALCULATE(
    COUNTROWS(vaccine_introduction),
    FILTER(
        vaccine_introduction,
        LOWER(vaccine_introduction[intro]) IN {"1","yes","y","true","introduced"}
    )
)

Coverage Achievement % =
DIVIDE([Average Coverage %], 95, 0)

Year-over-Year Coverage =
VAR CurrentYear = MAX(coverage[year])
VAR CurrentValue = [Average Coverage %]
VAR PreviousValue =
    CALCULATE(
        [Average Coverage %],
        FILTER(ALL(coverage[year]), coverage[year] = CurrentYear - 1)
    )
RETURN CurrentValue - PreviousValue

Recommended visuals:
1. KPI cards: Average Coverage %, Total Doses, Total Reported Cases, Coverage Gap to 95%.
2. Line chart: Year vs Average Coverage %.
3. Filled map: Country vs Average Coverage %.
4. Bar chart: Disease vs Total Reported Cases.
5. Scatter: Average Coverage % vs Average Incidence Rate, Country as detail.
6. Matrix: WHO Region x Vaccine Description x Introduction Year.
7. Slicers: Year, Country, WHO Region, Antigen, Disease.
